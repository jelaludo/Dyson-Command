    HOW TO PLAY

    Drag ships. Connect light. The clock runs itself.

    You are the green team. Build four planets and connect enough starlight to win. Your ships, power and enemy garrisons change while you play; there is no Wait button.

    FRONTIER GOAL4 built planets + 75 light output = winThe two counters at the top must reach their targets together.

    01 / TAKE A PLANET

    Drag ships to it.

    1. Press your green planet Eos. Drag to Iona. Release on Iona: the ships launch immediately.
    2. The slider marked Ships per drag chooses how many leave. It starts at 50%, so Eos sends 9 of its 18 ships. Send more ships than the defender number on the target.
    3. Watch the little fleet move. When it arrives, Iona becomes your green foothold. The clock, shipyards and battery continue automatically.
    4. Click captured Iona once to terraform it. That costs 40 power and makes it a built world with a working receiver.

    02 / GIVE IT LIGHT

    Drag a collector to it.

    The four gold diamonds near the star are collectors. Each built planet has a receiver, like a socket for one beam.

    Press SEED C and drag its beam to built Iona. When Iona glows, release. The beam snaps in and your Light output rises.

    Light output is your charging speed. Stored power is your battery. The battery gains that output every 2 seconds. Terraforming spends from it.

    If it does not snap, that collector cannot reach the planet, the receiver is not built, or another beam already uses it.

    03 / FINISH THE NETWORK

    Repeat: capture → build → connect.

    StartEos + Iona

    Eos already has beam A. Capture and build Iona; connect SEED C to it.

    NextTalus

    Drag ships from Eos to Talus. Use the slider if you need a larger fleet. Click captured Talus to terraform; connect HEL B.

    LastKora

    Drag ships from Talus to Kora. Terraform Kora; connect SEED D. Four different planets now receive four beams.

    That arrangement makes 82 light output. With four built planets, the campaign ends automatically.

    OTHER MODE

    Just the light puzzle?

    Choose Optics challenge. Eos, Iona, Talus and Kora are already built. Drag HEL B → Talus, SEED C → Iona and SEED D → Kora. HEL A already lights Eos. Reach 80 output to win.

    OTHER ACTIONS

    One click when needed.

    Click a captured foothold to terraform when you have 40 power. Click a red rival planet to reveal its optional SOL laser button. A laser costs 24 power and removes up to 7 defenders; it never captures a planet by itself.

    THE MAP

    What do the colors mean?

    Green planets belong to you, blue are neutral, red are rival. A dashed green planet is captured but still needs terraforming. The number under a planet is its current ship garrison. Built green planets make ships automatically every 2 seconds.

    THE CLOCK

    What updates by itself?

    Each 2-second cycle adds each built planet’s ship production and the current Light output to Stored power (up to 240). Rival garrisons gain one ship every other cycle. Fleet travel takes a few seconds based on distance. Launching, terraforming, aiming and firing do not advance the clock.

    THE BEAMS

    Which collector reaches which planet?

    CollectorOutputReachable planets

    Only your built planets have working receivers. One receiver takes one beam. Drag near a valid receiver until it glows, then release. An invalid drop leaves the old alignment alone.

    COMBAT

    What happens on arrival?

    A fleet wins only if it carries more ships than the target has defenders when it arrives. Survivors become the new garrison. An equal-size fleet removes the defenders but does not capture the world. A fleet arriving at one of your planets reinforces it.

    DESIGN PROTOTYPE

    A live system of fleets and light.

    Fleets take a foothold. Terraforming makes it productive and installs a receiver. Optical alignment raises stellar power; the SOL laser spends stored power to weaken a defended rival. Ships and power grow as time passes.
